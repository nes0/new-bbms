Active and honorary members without a key who have come in at least once, most hours in first. There's no threshold: once the checks are done, whether to trust someone and give them a key is your decision. Tick Trusted on the member's page (it emails them), and Key holder when they collect their key.

A visit is a day their fob was used at the door or on a machine. Hours in are the same estimate as Who's in: from their first tap until they tap out, or {{ config.occupancy_timeout_hours }} hour{{ config.occupancy_timeout_hours|pluralize }} after their last fob use if they don't. Trusted members in at the same time are those whose estimated time in the space overlapped theirs, so they will have seen them at work.
