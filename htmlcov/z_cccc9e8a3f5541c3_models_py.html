1""" 

2Bank statements imported by finance (spec 04 §4.6, §4.9), and the remembered matches between bank descriptions and 

3members. How a statement is read and matched is in `statements.py`. 

4 

5A statement is uploaded as a **draft**: BBMS reads its credits and suggests a member and a reason for each, and 

6nothing is recorded until finance has checked them and pressed "Record". Every credit line is then kept, matched or 

7not, so differences with the bank are listed rather than hidden. 

8""" 

9

128class Payout(models.Model): 

129 """A lump sum GoCardless or Stripe paid into the bank: the payments inside it, less their fees (spec 04 §4.9). 

130 

131 Imported from the provider's API (`payouts.py`), so the real fees are known and each bank statement line from a 

132 provider can be matched to the payments it is made of. 

133 """
