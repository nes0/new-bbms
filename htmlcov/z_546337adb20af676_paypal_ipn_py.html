1""" 

2What each PayPal notification (IPN) does (spec 04 §4.4). 

3 

4The view checks a notification with PayPal, then `store` keeps it (ignoring a repeat) and `process_pending` handles 

5the stored ones in the order they arrived. One that fails is kept with its error for someone to look at (admin: 

6Payments → PayPal notifications, "try again"). 

7 

8| PayPal says | What happens | 

9|---|---| 

10| `subscr_signup` | The member pays by PayPal from now on; one still setting up (or leaving) is activated. | 

11| `subscr_payment`, `Completed` | Recorded once: pays the oldest due or failed subscription charge, or goes to credit. | 

12| `web_accept`, `Completed` | A credit top-up, recorded once. | 

13| `Refunded`, `Reversed`, `Canceled_Reversal` | A correcting entry linked to the original payment; finance is told. | 

14| `subscr_failed` | The member is emailed; PayPal tries again by itself. | 

15| `subscr_cancel`, `subscr_eot` | A member paying by that subscription starts leaving. | 

16 

17Someone BBMS can't match is recorded as a donation with no member; finance is told and the payer is emailed. A 

18member who has left isn't rejoined: the money goes to their credit and finance is told. 

19"""
